Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Mara Lenti application ("App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account to Mara Lenti, we receive limited profile information (display name, follower count) and, only with your permission, the ability to post content to your account on your behalf.

We do not collect passwords, payment data, or sensitive personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to authenticate your TikTok account via OAuth 2.0, post or schedule content you have approved, and display analytics about your TikTok performance.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with TikTok as required to operate the integration described above.

4. Data Retention

Your TikTok access tokens are stored locally on your device. We retain no server-side copy. You may revoke access at any time from TikTok's connected apps settings.

5. Security

We store all credentials in a local .env file on your own machine. You are responsible for keeping this file secure.
